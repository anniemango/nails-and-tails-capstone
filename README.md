# Nails and Tails GIT 337 Capstone

A four-page site for a fictional Tempe salon that does your manicure and your dog's grooming in the same visit.

**Audience:** busy dog owners in the Tempe area who want their own nail appointment and their dog's groom in one trip.

**Priority tasks**
1. Understand a combined visit and its price (Home).
2. Request a visit (Book a Visit).
3. Find hours, what to bring, and the cancellation policy (Visit and Support).

Before you start

Required fields are marked "(required)" in their label. Everything else is optional and you can leave it blank.

We only ask for what we need to hold the appointment and reach you about it. We do not ask for your address, your dog's medical records, or payment details.

What the form checks

What the form checks before it will send, and what it deliberately leaves alone.

  • You must fill in your first name, email address, your dog's name and size, the service, a preferred date, and a time window.
  • Email is checked for format validity. Example: name@example.com
  • Preferred date takes any day from 13 October 2026 to 9 January 2027.
  • Length limits are 40 characters for the name fields and 500 for the notes box.
  • No rigid patterns. Names hold apostrophes, hyphens, spaces and letters from any alphabet, so a pattern would only turn real people away. No phone number is asked for at all.

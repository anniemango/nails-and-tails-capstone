Plan your visit

We're at 1200 Mill Avenue, Tempe, Arizona, with free parking behind the building. Leashed dogs are welcome in the lobby.

Hours

  • Tuesday to Friday: 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Saturday: 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.
  • Sunday and Monday: closed

Book an appointment

Call (480) 555-0123 to book a nail and grooming visit together. New clients can use code PAWS10 for 10% off the first visit.

New client checklist

  • Bring your dog's current rabies vaccination record.
  • Tell us about any allergies, for you or your pet.
  • Arrive 10 minutes early to fill out the intake form.

Cancellation policy

Please cancel at least 24 hours ahead. Late cancellations may be charged half the service price.
